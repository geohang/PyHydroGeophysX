No-Name hillslope (Snowy Range, Wyoming) time-lapse ERT
=======================================================

Source
------
Five DAS-1 acquisitions, 5 to 9 November 2017, from the time-lapse ERT
monitoring of the No-Name hillslope, Snowy Range, Medicine Bow National Forest,
Wyoming, by Andrew Parsekian's group at the University of Wyoming.

The group publishes this monitoring series as:
Parsekian, A. (2020). NoName_Snowy_Range_MBNF_Hydrogeophysics. HydroShare.
http://www.hydroshare.org/resource/3357f6ea99ca48a4b418a0be14fb653b
