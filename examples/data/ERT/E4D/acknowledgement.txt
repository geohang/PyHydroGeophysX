Chicken Bone Meadow (Mt. Snodgrass, Crested Butte, Colorado) time-lapse ERT
============================================================================

Source
------
Uhlemann, S., Dafflon, B., Chou, C., Wielandt, S., Wu, Y., and Williams, K.
(2024). Geophysical and Environmental Monitoring Data, and Subsurface Flow
Modelling Results for Chicken Bone Meadow, Mt. Snodgrass, Crested Butte, CO.
ESS-DIVE (Environmental System Science Data Infrastructure for a Virtual
Ecosystem); Watershed Function SFA.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.15485/2283406

The ten .ohm files here are a subset of the ERT acquisitions in the release's
FieldData.zip (October 2021 to June 2022), in E4D's survey format.
