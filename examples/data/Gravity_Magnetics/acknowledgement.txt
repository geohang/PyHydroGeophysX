This folder holds two open-source potential-field demo datasets, reformatted to
the workbench station format (x_m, y_m, value). Local x/y in metres come from an
equirectangular projection of the original longitude/latitude; the anomaly values
are unchanged.

==============================================================================
1. bushveld_gravity_disturbance.csv   (gravity, mGal)
==============================================================================
Ground gravity disturbance over the Bushveld Igneous Complex, Southern Africa
(3877 stations).

Source : Southern Africa gravity compilation, NOAA NCEI
         (https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/mgg/gravity/); original data public domain.
         Curated, and the gravity disturbance computed, by the Fatiando a Terra
         project (ensaio).
DOI    : https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.6511942
License: CC-BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
Link   : https://github.com/fatiando-data/bushveld-gravity

==============================================================================
2. britain_aeromagnetic_anomaly.csv   (magnetics, nT)
==============================================================================
Airborne total-field magnetic anomaly over Britain. A 1x1 degree subset over
south-west England (about 3451 stations) of the full survey is included here so
the demo file stays small.

Source : British Geological Survey, GB aeromagnetic survey
         (https://www.bgs.ac.uk/datasets/gb-aeromagnetic-survey/).
         Contains British Geological Survey materials (c) UKRI 2021,
         Open Government Licence. Curated by the Fatiando a Terra project (ensaio).
DOI    : https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.5879260
License: CC-BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
Link   : https://github.com/fatiando-data/britain-magnetic

We thank NOAA NCEI, the British Geological Survey, and the Fatiando a Terra
project for making these datasets openly available.
