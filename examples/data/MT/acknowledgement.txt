NMX20.xml : magnetotelluric transfer functions of USMTArray station NMX20
(Nations Draw, New Mexico, USA; 34.4705 N, 108.7123 W, 1940 m), in EMTF XML.

The station was recorded with NIMS long-period instruments from 2020-09-20 to
2020-10-07 (runs NMX20a, NMX20b) and processed with remote reference; the file
holds the impedance and tipper at 33 periods from 4.7 s to 29 000 s, with their
full error covariance. Read it with
PyHydroGeophysX.data_processing.mt.read_transfer_function.

Source   : IRIS/SAGE SPUD EMTF repository, http://ds.iris.edu/spud/emtf
Dataset  : Schultz, A., Pellerin, L., Bedrosian, P., Kelbert, A., Crosbie, J.
           (2020-2023). USMTArray South Magnetotelluric Transfer Functions.
           Seismological Facility for the Advancement of Geoscience.
           https://doi.org/10.17611/DP/EMTF/USMTARRAY/SOUTH
