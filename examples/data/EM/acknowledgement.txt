skytem_bhmar_tdem.csv : airborne time-domain EM (SkyTEM) demo sounding line.

Five soundings from a real SkyTEM survey flown for the Broken Hill Managed
Aquifer Recharge (BHMAR) project, New South Wales, Australia. The values are the
high-moment (HM) vertical dB/dt (V/A.m^4). Column 1 is the gate centre time in
seconds, taken from the SkyTEM-HM system file (21 gates, about 86 us to 8.8 ms);
columns 2 to 6 are five soundings spread along the flight line. Load any one of
them with the "Sounding #" picker in the EM panel.

skytem_bhmar_geometry.csv : along-line geometry for these five soundings. The
easting/northing come from the data column headers (real MGA coordinates) and
dist_m is the cumulative along-line distance. The soundings are about 12 km apart
(a coarse regional transect, ~48 km total). Load it with "Load geometry..." in the
EM panel to place the section on real distance instead of uniform spacing.

Dataset: Brodie, R.C. (2019). Broken Hill Managed Aquifer Recharge (BHMAR)
         SkyTEM Airborne Electromagnetic Survey, NSW, 2009. Commonwealth of
         Australia (Geoscience Australia).
