Then choose a direction
-----------------------

- :doc:`Workflows <tutorials/index>` if you are starting from your data: a
  hydrological model, a field survey, or repeated surveys.
- :doc:`Methods <methods/index>` if you already know which measurement you are
  working with.
- :doc:`Examples <examples/index>` for complete, downloadable scripts.

What the package is for
-----------------------

Information travels in both directions between a hydrological model and a
geophysical survey. A model state predicts what a survey should measure, and a
survey constrains what the model should hold.
:doc:`Connecting geophysical data and hydrological models
<tutorials/hydro_geophysical_interaction>` walks that connection end to end and
is the best single page to read before choosing a workflow.
