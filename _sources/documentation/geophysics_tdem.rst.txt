Geophysics: TDEM
================

Time-Domain Electromagnetics (TDEM) workflows use SimPEG to generate forward
responses and perform inversions from hydro-derived conductivity models.

Use Cases
---------

- Compare EM responses to hydrologic changes
- Validate petrophysical parameters with EM data
